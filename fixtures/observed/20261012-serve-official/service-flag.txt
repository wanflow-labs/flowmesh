$ tailscale serve --bg --service=svc:web --http=80 3000
This machine is configured as a service proxy for svc:web, but approval from an admin is required. Once approved, it will be available in your Tailnet as:

http://web.mesh.lab.internal/
|-- proxy http://127.0.0.1:3000

Serve started and running in the background.
To disable the proxy, run: tailscale serve --service=svc:web --http=80 off
To remove config for the service, run: tailscale serve clear svc:web
[exit 0]
